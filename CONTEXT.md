# Chat host control

- **Host invocation**: the one browser POST already owned by a unique turn-stream object, including its detached drain and resource disposal. It is not a retry ledger or a native execution/completion owner. _Avoid_: job, admission ledger.
- **Host control receipt**: the opt-in address of that invocation, issued after identity/editor/actual-thread authorization. It grants no execution authority and does not mean paid admission or assistant output. _Avoid_: accepted turn, execution receipt.
- **Accepted host Stop**: monotonic cancellation intent for that live invocation, retained until its owned work ends. It does not confirm final settlement or suppress future independent HTTP submissions with the same client IDs. _Avoid_: stopped execution, permanent replay tombstone.
