# Agent app lessons

Every agent app built on agent-app inherits these lessons.
GTM learned each one in production on 2026-10-09 and 2026-10-10, mostly by fixing it by hand.
Before you build or change an app, check the lessons that touch your change, and rely on the enforcing checks instead of copying their fixes.

Each row gives the lesson, the incident that taught it, and the check that enforces it.
Checks marked *launch kit* are the nine invariants in agent-app's `src/launch-invariants/` ([#963](https://github.com/tangle-network/agent-app/pull/963), [#975](https://github.com/tangle-network/agent-app/pull/975)).
They run against each app's own routes and jobs. Every app runs them as `pnpm run invariants` (in its signoff where it has one), whose report out of 9 is that app's current status; `docs/launch-invariants.md` is the adoption guide.
Checks marked *in review* are open pull requests, and checks marked *planned* do not exist yet.
Until one merges, the lesson is a review rule.

Repositories: [GTM](https://github.com/tangle-network/gtm-agent), [ADC](https://github.com/tangle-network/agent-container) (agent-container), [dotfiles](https://github.com/drewstone/dotfiles) (operator tooling).

## Data and load

| Lesson | Taught by | Enforced by |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Bounded reads and memory budgets.** A request or job reads a bounded batch and stays inside a declared memory budget, whatever the table size. | Unbounded reads over 1.63M stream events overloaded D1 and broke sign-in at 18:01Z and 18:54Z. Worker memory limits were hit 6 times, by chat and hourly crons. | *launch kit* `bounded-reads` (`sizedBatches` in `sized-reads.ts`) and `memory-budget` (`checkScheduledJobBudgets`: heap growth at most 40 MB, every scheduled job covered). Fixes: [`tests/turn-health/bounded-parts.test.ts`](../tests/turn-health/bounded-parts.test.ts) (#953), GTM `tests/reliability-scorecard.test.ts` ([#1484](https://github.com/tangle-network/gtm-agent/pull/1484)), [#1426](https://github.com/tangle-network/gtm-agent/pull/1426) (queue bounded by size), [#1465](https://github.com/tangle-network/gtm-agent/pull/1465) (Worker chunks). |
| **Coalesced events.** Coalesce a model's token stream before it is persisted; never write one row per token. | A reasoning model's per-token events filled the turn-stream tables, whose 1.63M events then overloaded D1. | *launch kit* `coalesced-events` (`checkCoalescedTurn` on a recorded 59,659-event turn); [`tests/turn-buffer.test.ts`](../tests/turn-buffer.test.ts) (#946). |
| **Isolated scheduled jobs.** Each cron sweep runs alone, with retries and its own budget, so one heavy sweep cannot starve or crash the others. | Hourly crons at 13:01, 14:01, 17:01, 21:00 and 22:00Z hit the Worker memory limit together; turn recovery ran only hourly. | *launch kit* `isolated-jobs` (`createScheduledDispatch` and `checkIsolatedJobs` in `schedule.ts`: one job per scheduled invocation); GTM `tests/cron-triggers.test.ts` and `tests/orphaned-turn-streams.test.ts` ([#1496](https://github.com/tangle-network/gtm-agent/pull/1496), [#1500](https://github.com/tangle-network/gtm-agent/pull/1500), [#1504](https://github.com/tangle-network/gtm-agent/pull/1504)). |
| **Limits alarm before they bind.** Every resource has a declared budget, a test, and an alarm at 80%. | 19 limit hits in one day: Worker memory, D1, sandbox disk, snapshot cap, operator-key rate, agent spend, Beelink disk. | *launch kit* `limit-alarms` (`createLimitAlarms` in `limit-alarms.ts`: worker memory, D1 rows per query, sandbox disk, snapshot count, key rate; silent at 79%, warns at 80%, pages at 100%). |

## Turns

| Lesson | Taught by | Enforced by |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Honest settlement, with detection lag.** A turn that cannot finish settles as an honest failure with Retry, attributed to its request time. Report the lag from failure to notice as a metric of its own. Never re-run a turn automatically, and never move a settled turn in time. | A failure was dated by its 19:43Z notice instead of its 09:13Z request, a 10.5 h detection lag that no metric showed. | *launch kit* `honest-settlement` (`checkTurnSettlement`; `settleOrphanedTurns` in `src/stream/orphaned-turns.ts` returns the detection lag, and an app that settles more than 15 min after a lease expires fails); [`src/stream/turn-buffer-d1.test.ts`](../src/stream/turn-buffer-d1.test.ts) (#951), native-completion #940, #941, #942; GTM `tests/reliability-scorecard.test.ts` ([#1501](https://github.com/tangle-network/gtm-agent/pull/1501), [#1481](https://github.com/tangle-network/gtm-agent/pull/1481), which reports a person's Stop apart from failures). |
| **Restart-lossless deploys, with a canary.** An orchestrator restart or deploy never kills running work. Every deploy runs a canary turn and rolls back on failure. | The orchestrator's crash recovery stopped the b81b box with 6 running turns (`inc-20261010-idle-reaper-kill`). Two more turns were lost after the first fix. | ADC `orchestrator-lifecycle-live-work.test.ts` ([ADC #10047](https://github.com/tangle-network/agent-container/pull/10047)); restart drill canary (PASS 22:15Z); GTM `scripts/ship.sh release`, which runs a startup canary, migrations, a served-revision check, a chat canary and automatic rollback. |

## Auth and the edge

| Lesson | Taught by | Enforced by |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Auth survives a D1 stall.** A just-verified key or session keeps working for up to 60 s while D1 cannot answer, and revocation stays honored. | API-key lookup and email sign-in failed during the D1 overload, at 18:01Z and 18:54:56Z. | *launch kit* `auth-survives-d1-stall` (`createAuthLookupCache` in `auth-lookup-cache.ts`, promoted from GTM); GTM `tests/auth-lookup-cache.test.ts` ([#1488](https://github.com/tangle-network/gtm-agent/pull/1488)). |
| **Auth before stream.** Authorize a turn before its stream opens. A progress-first response must not run ahead of the access check. | Speeding up first visible progress opened turn streams before `authorize` had run. | *launch kit* `authorize-before-stream` (`checkAuthorizeBeforeStream`, per app); [`tests/chat-routes/authorize-before-stream.test.ts`](../tests/chat-routes/authorize-before-stream.test.ts) (real HTTP identity/model boundaries and SQLite effects, normal and progress-first turns). |
| **Fetch through the app's fetch tool, not the box's HTTP clients.** The app's fetch tool is the supported way for an agent to read web pages. Never spoof a browser, never weaken a zone to let a client through, and never patch the sandbox's clients from the per-turn env. | Python's `Python-urllib/3.x` User-Agent gets 403 from bot checks on many sites, Tangle's own included. [#1499](https://github.com/tangle-network/gtm-agent/pull/1499) set `PYTHONPATH` and `CURL_HOME` to inject an honest one; the sidecar refuses `PYTHONPATH` as a reserved name, the release's chat canary failed at provisioning, and [#1515](https://github.com/tangle-network/gtm-agent/pull/1515) reverted it. The box's Nix Python ignores user site files and HOME is new each execution, so decision D10 closed with no in-box fix. | *planned*: a test that checks every per-turn env name against ADC's `FORBIDDEN_ENV_VAR_NAMES` (`packages/runtime-contracts`), and a sandbox env change proven on a real sidecar, never host Python alone. |
| **Pasted code reaches chat.** The WAF's managed OWASP rules do not run on authenticated chat-message routes. Rate limiting and bot and DDoS protection stay on. | Code pasted into a chat message matched the managed OWASP signatures, and the edge refused the message (decision D23). On 2026-10-11 the zone still blocked 2 of 6 pasted-code samples (`cat /etc/passwd`, `../../etc/hosts`) on POST `/api/chat` with its 403 page, so the D23 skip is not in effect. | *launch kit* `pasted-code-reaches-chat` (`PASTED_CODE_SAMPLES`, `isEdgeBlock`, `checkChatAcceptsCode`; [#975](https://github.com/tangle-network/agent-app/pull/975)); GTM `scripts/chat-edge-probe.mjs` ([#1519](https://github.com/tangle-network/gtm-agent/pull/1519)), a post-deploy step of `scripts/ship.sh release` that posts the samples without credentials to every message route (`/api/chat`, `/api/chat/interactions`, the operator turns route, the new-chat form) and fails the release, without a rollback, on a block page. Since zone rule `gtm_chat_command_text_v1` v3 (01:07:45Z on 2026-10-11), 24 of 24 reach the origin. The samples are a floor: other Cloudflare Managed rules still block pastes such as `${jndi:...}`, `'); DROP TABLE`, `<?php system(...)`, `onerror=` and `nc -e`, and widening the skip is a policy call. |
| **Never share a promise across requests.** A module-level cache or single-flight map may hold a *resolved* value across requests, never a promise still in flight. On Workers, a promise's I/O belongs to the request that started it; when that request is cancelled (the member navigates), the promise never settles, and every other request awaiting it hangs until its client gives up. Concurrent requests each do their own work, or answer at once that the work is already running. | Hospitality, 2026-10-11. #966's isolate session cache shared an in-flight session read: after a cancelled navigation, `/api/workspaces`, `/models` and `/api/auth/tangle/status` hung 20.4–21.6 s (3 of 639 status calls), the composer never rendered, the canary failed and two releases rolled back. `createWorkspacePrewarmRoute` had the same shape: joined prewarms hung 43–45 s. | [`tests/app-auth.test.ts`](../tests/app-auth.test.ts) "never shares a read in flight across requests" ([#993](https://github.com/tangle-network/agent-app/pull/993)); [`src/sandbox/prewarm-route.test.ts`](../src/sandbox/prewarm-route.test.ts) "answers joined at once for a warm already running for the same key, never awaiting it" ([#995](https://github.com/tangle-network/agent-app/pull/995)). *planned*: a lint that flags a module-level `Map` or `WeakMap` whose values are promises. |

## Sandboxes

| Lesson | Taught by | Enforced by |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **A planned host restart is not a dead sandbox.** Honor the platform's typed `HOST_AGENT_RESTARTING` refusal during liveness and session reads, bounded by the existing provisioning timeout. Recheck the same runtime after the host returns; never stop or replace it because the planned-restart wait expired. | GTM, 2026-10-22: liveness recovery attempted to suspend an existing sandbox while its host-agent was restarting. The transport and egress counter were unavailable, so accounting correctly refused the suspend and the turn failed. | [`tests/sandbox-planned-host-restart.integration.test.ts`](../tests/sandbox-planned-host-restart.integration.test.ts): real Sandbox SDK over loopback HTTP, typed probe/session refusals, bounded expiry, and unrelated refusal control. |
| **Sandbox disk budgets.** Everything the runtime writes per turn has a cap and a pruner, and shows up in the box's usage report. A full box names itself instead of asking for a retry. | The b81b box ran out of its 10 GB disk and every turn failed with ENOSPC. The causes were an OpenCode HOME copied per turn (65 MB) and a process-I/O spool about 5.6x turn output, both hidden from `/files/usage`. | *launch kit* `limit-alarms` (sandbox disk); ADC `opencode-idle-home-reclaim.test.ts` ([ADC #10088](https://github.com/tangle-network/agent-container/pull/10088)), `native-settled-retention.test.ts` ([ADC #10068](https://github.com/tangle-network/agent-container/pull/10068)); GTM `tests/box-storage-headroom.test.ts` ([#1458](https://github.com/tangle-network/gtm-agent/pull/1458)); a process-I/O ring buffer (64 MB per runtime folder, 1 GB per box), in progress in ADC. |
| **Snapshot retention.** Platform snapshots have their own retention outside the customer's cap. A failed snapshot alarms. Recovery restores from the newest snapshot that exists, and a restore drill proves it. | b81b's newest snapshot was 3 days old, the error was recorded without a log line, and the restore pointer named a deleted snapshot. Retention also pruned customer snapshots inside a live session. | *launch kit* `limit-alarms` (snapshot count); ADC `snapshot-deferred-delete.test.ts`, `restic-snapshot-count.test.ts` ([ADC #10082](https://github.com/tangle-network/agent-container/pull/10082)); GTM `tests/snapshot-rotation.test.ts` ([#1463](https://github.com/tangle-network/gtm-agent/pull/1463)), `tests/snapshot-resolve.test.ts` and the restore drill ([#1469](https://github.com/tangle-network/gtm-agent/pull/1469), [#1476](https://github.com/tangle-network/gtm-agent/pull/1476)). |
| **Key a cache on what the live payload carries, and do not hold request-spanning state in isolate memory.** A Worker request is not promised the isolate that served the last one. State a reuse trusts is keyed on fields the platform returns today and lives in a store the next isolate reads. | agent-app [#1175](https://github.com/tangle-network/agent-app/pull/1175) keyed every cached check on `box.startedAt`; the Sandbox API returns none on a list row or a get, so the old egress cache never hit either. [#1177](https://github.com/tangle-network/agent-app/pull/1177) re-keyed it on the filesystem incarnation, and eight consecutive Legal turns still found no check from the turn before, each in a fresh isolate. Reuse claims fell from p50 2,449 ms to 871 ms only after [#1178](https://github.com/tangle-network/agent-app/pull/1178). | `ReuseStateStore` over the Workers Cache API (`src/sandbox/reuse-state.ts`), exercised across a simulated isolate switch in `src/sandbox/index.test.ts`; read a live payload (`GET /v1/sandboxes/{id}`) before keying a cache on a field. |

## Computer steps

A computer step is the box work a Router turn asks for through `sandbox_run`. GTM's Router-first `sandbox_run` failed on every production call from [#1908](https://github.com/tangle-network/gtm-agent/pull/1908) on 2026-10-24, through three causes in turn; each rule below is one of them.

| Lesson | Taught by | Enforced by |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **One program, one copy, under the request cap.** Mount a program that answers to several names once, and give the other names `aliasOf`. A secret-isolated dispatch carries every file mount inline in one request capped at 1 MiB; one that cannot fit is refused, naming its largest mounts, before it is sent. | GTM, 2026-10-24 ([#1919](https://github.com/tangle-network/gtm-agent/pull/1919)): one 342 KB program mounted under five names made a 2,065,490-byte isolated profile against the 1,048,576-byte sandbox gateway cap, and every computer child failed 33 ms after `box_ready`. | `buildSandboxToolFileMounts` (`aliasOf` mounts a shim that runs the program under its own name; two copies of a program of 4 KB or more throw) and `assertIsolatedDispatchWithinCap`, which `streamSandboxPrompt` and `driveSandboxTurn` run on every isolated dispatch; [`tests/sandbox/tool-mount-alias.integration.test.ts`](../tests/sandbox/tool-mount-alias.integration.test.ts) (real `/bin/sh` and `python3`), [`tests/chat-routes/default-assembly.integration.test.ts`](../tests/chat-routes/default-assembly.integration.test.ts) (real Sandbox SDK: the over-cap dispatch sends nothing and leaves the workspace free; the aliased profile fits). |
| **A computer step's session mode is one input, and its send witness works in every mode.** Pass `secretProfileIsolation` from the same value that created the session, never from a second flag. Pass `onDispatch` in a shared and an isolated session alike: agent-app calls it at its own send, so a failure before it fires was never sent, and one after it may have started a run. | GTM, 2026-10-24: the child required an isolated session while production ran with isolation off, so every child was refused ([#1921](https://github.com/tangle-network/gtm-agent/pull/1921)); then the shared child passed a send observer that only GTM's isolated admission supported, and every child was recorded as never dispatched ([#1922](https://github.com/tangle-network/gtm-agent/pull/1922)). | `StreamSandboxPromptOptions.onDispatch` on the streaming, detached and `driveSandboxTurn` sends; `sandboxPromptRunner` reports a failure before its witness as `sandbox_unavailable`, so the workspace is not held for a run that never started; [`tests/chat-routes/default-assembly.integration.test.ts`](../tests/chat-routes/default-assembly.integration.test.ts) (shared and detached witness; a refusal before the send releases the workspace). |
| **A computer failure's cause is in the transcript.** The redacted cause chain of a failed computer step is kept with the turn, where operators and support read it, not only in a log line. | GTM, 2026-10-24: causes went only to `console.error`, which Workers Logs did not keep, so failing threads read only `sandbox_failed` until [#1920](https://github.com/tangle-network/gtm-agent/pull/1920) wrote them to D1. | `createHybridTurnProducer` persists `sandboxRunFailureDetail` as the warning notice's `detail` and logs it when the app passes no `onFailure`; [`tests/chat-routes/hybrid-fallback.integration.test.ts`](../tests/chat-routes/hybrid-fallback.integration.test.ts) (real SDK refusals, credentials redacted). *in review*: the box's own failure text in `runSandboxPrompt`'s error ([#1319](https://github.com/tangle-network/agent-app/pull/1319)). |
| **Every release proves a computer turn.** The release canary runs a plain turn and a turn only the workspace computer can answer, and fails the release when the computer step fails, naming its recorded cause. | Each of the three causes above reached production and was found from failing production threads (#1919 to #1922 cite them). | The create-agent-app chat template's `scripts/release-canary.mjs`, which `pnpm deploy` runs after `wrangler deploy`, and its `tests/release-canary.e2e.test.ts`. *planned*: the same two turns in each existing app's release canary (GTM `scripts/chat-canary.mjs`, Creative, Legal). |

## Delivery

| Lesson | Taught by | Enforced by |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **The gate is ship verify.** The pre-merge gate runs exactly the command the deploy runs, so nothing reaches master that the deploy will refuse. | 4 deploys failed on checks the gate never ran: lint at 14:13, 22:03 and 22:14Z, and agent-eval peer floors at 20:25Z ([#1445](https://github.com/tangle-network/gtm-agent/pull/1445), reverted in [#1448](https://github.com/tangle-network/gtm-agent/pull/1448)). | GTM `signoff.config.mjs` and `scripts/ship-verify.mjs`, through `bash scripts/ship.sh verify`; dotfiles `beelink-gate --ship`, which runs exactly that command before a merge ([#325](https://github.com/drewstone/dotfiles/pull/325), [#330](https://github.com/drewstone/dotfiles/pull/330)). |
| **A docs-only merge must not skip a release.** A docs-only merge to a deploy branch mid-Verify skips the release, because the deploy's freshness check sees a newer master. So ledger rows ride with code PRs, and deploys filter out paths that do not touch the runtime. | Twice in one night, [#1509](https://github.com/tangle-network/gtm-agent/pull/1509) and [#1510](https://github.com/tangle-network/gtm-agent/pull/1510) (climb ledger rows) landed while a verified commit was in Verify, and its release was skipped. 38 verified runs released 3. | GTM `.github/workflows/deploy.yml` `paths-ignore` for non-runtime paths ([#1512](https://github.com/tangle-network/gtm-agent/pull/1512)); `scripts/ship.sh` `release_is_forward` and one `deploy-production` concurrency group, so every verified commit ahead of production releases, with its harness case ([#1513](https://github.com/tangle-network/gtm-agent/pull/1513)). Ledgers merge by union (`.gitattributes` `merge=union`, [#1524](https://github.com/tangle-network/gtm-agent/pull/1524)), and dotfiles `climb-log check` rejects a split, interleaved or repeated row ([#332](https://github.com/drewstone/dotfiles/pull/332)); GitHub's own merge ignores the attribute, so a conflicting branch is updated locally. Git reads attributes from the checked-out branch, so a branch cut before `.gitattributes` named the ledger still conflicted (L1's chain 10, 01:51Z on 2026-10-11); dotfiles `git/install.sh` now sets a global attributes file with the same lines on every host ([#334](https://github.com/drewstone/dotfiles/pull/334), [#335](https://github.com/drewstone/dotfiles/pull/335)). |
| **No single coordination host.** Unattended chains, gate entry points and lock holders each need a second home, and a lock dies with its holder. | gtr went offline at about 23:39Z, taking the gate entry point and the unattended chains with it. Earlier, gate lock waits reached 72 min. | dotfiles `beelink-gate --ship auto`, which starts from either Beelink on whichever has a free slot, with launcher-death and hold-limit proofs ([#325](https://github.com/drewstone/dotfiles/pull/325), [#330](https://github.com/drewstone/dotfiles/pull/330)). |
| **One shared key, one budget.** Every client of a shared operator key draws from one per-key request budget on its host and waits out `Retry-After`, instead of retrying into the limit. | Parallel lanes on one GTM operator key sent 1,121 requests and got 653 429s; flow-agent polling then spent the key's 1,000-request daily quota, and every gtm-ask failed until 00:00Z (`inc-20261010-operator-key-quota`). | dotfiles `claude/tools/operator-api/rate.mjs` and `Retry-After` in agent-ask and gtm-ask ([#331](https://github.com/drewstone/dotfiles/pull/331)): on 8 lanes against one 60/min key, 429s fell from 72 of 176 requests to 0 of 104. |
| **Bump a shared package in every pin at once.** An app can pin agent-app in up to three places: the dependency, `pnpm.overrides` and `minimumReleaseAgeExclude`. A bump changes all of them in one commit. | Launch-kit adoptions across 8 apps on 2026-10-11 failed the CI install whenever one of the three pins was missed. | *planned*: a check that the three pins name one version. |
| **Run-origin tags on test traffic.** Every eval, canary and agent-test request carries its run origin. Test leads and test turns run every step but count in no customer metric and notify no one. | Agent test turns emailed Drew 42 approval requests. Test submissions counted as leads. | GTM `tests/inbound-leads.test.ts` ([#1450](https://github.com/tangle-network/gtm-agent/pull/1450)); hosted-runner origin header ([#1491](https://github.com/tangle-network/gtm-agent/pull/1491)). |
| **No prompt-text tests.** Test behavior and safety, not the wording of a prompt, skill or page. Fix a habit-type failure with a runtime capability, not another prompt edit. | [#1436](https://github.com/tangle-network/gtm-agent/pull/1436) deleted copy and prompt-text tests that asserted wording, not behavior. After two prompt-edit rounds, only 3 of 30 research runs cut at 20 calls left a deliverable. | GTM `eslint-rules/no-prompt-text-assertions.js` ([#1519](https://github.com/tangle-network/gtm-agent/pull/1519)), run by `pnpm lint` in ship verify. Calibrated on GTM's 550 test files: 43 hits, 4 of them false positives (two command names, a label and a template carrying fixture data); a per-file baseline fails any new wording assertion while the existing ones are rewritten. |

## Measurement

| Lesson | Taught by | Enforced by |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Every number names its sample.** A reported number states its selection rule, sample size and window. A per-release number also states one code version, with a window that starts after that version deployed. Durations select by start time. | 5 numbers were retracted in one day. "Coalescing p50 32,401" chose streams by finish time across two releases, a failure time was measured from its notice, and a ~7 GB disk reclaim was an estimate. | dotfiles `climb-log append` and `climb-log check`, which refuse a ledger row without that measurement block; process-guard refuses shell writes into a ledger. |
| **Measure before naming a cause.** Name a cause only after a measurement separates it from the alternatives, such as a per-path usage diff or a replay that reproduces it. | 3 disk causes were named and retracted for one full box before a per-path measurement found the per-turn OpenCode HOME copy ([ADC #10088](https://github.com/tangle-network/agent-container/pull/10088)). | The climb's mechanism and red-team passes (dotfiles `docs/processes/climb.md`); `climb-log hills` lists each hill whose instrument lacks a calibration check. |
