`./agent-profiles` ships live profile self-mounting: `createMountProfileChangeTool` is the permitted, idempotent self-improve verb as a product-registerable app tool — only the `skills` dimension is changeable (model/permissions/billing refused with the permitted list), deltas are catalog-validated, deduped by identity, baseline-protected and mount-capped, with product-supplied storage and scope. `createInMemorySelfMountStorage` is the non-durable default.
