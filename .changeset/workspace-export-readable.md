Workspace exports an owner can read, and that wait out a busy sandbox. Sandbox archives leave out `node_modules` and `.sidecar` by default; tables with no rows write no file; a rows source with `format: "csv"` and `columns` writes `spreadsheets/<name>.csv`; `sandboxFiles` writes documents a box stores under internal names at their real names. A sandbox busy with another lifecycle operation (typed 409 `SANDBOX_LIFECYCLE_IN_PROGRESS`) is retried on a later request without spending an attempt (`ExportRetryLater`). Progress never counts a failed source as done (`unitsFailed`), the panel bar turns red on failure, and README.txt describes the folders the archive actually holds.
